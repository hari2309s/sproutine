# Sproutine

**Eat well, one sprout at a time.**

A voice-first healthy eating companion that logs your meals, plans your week and nudges you gently, without the guilt.

Sproutine is an AI-native nutrition companion. Log meals by voice, text, barcode or photo, get a weekly plan that respects your allergies, diet and budget, ask nutrition questions with cited answers, and get gentle nudges that help habits stick. Your default guide is **Rosie** (from *Rosenkohl*, German for Brussels sprouts).

Built for people making a fresh start, such as after quitting smoking, and tuned for shopping at German supermarkets.

> Sproutine offers wellness guidance, not medical advice.

## Stack

Next.js · React · Tailwind · LangGraph.js · Vercel AI SDK · Supabase (Postgres, pgvector, Auth) · Langfuse · PostHog. It runs entirely on free tiers, with a multi-provider LLM router across Groq, Gemini, Cerebras, OpenRouter and Mistral.
